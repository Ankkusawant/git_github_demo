Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 September 2026

G-7 (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is a business operated by Ankush Sawant, based in Pune, Maharashtra, India. We provide website development, business automation, and related digital services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, retain, and protect information provided through the G-7 website and during communications relating to our services.

Information we collect

When you contact us through our enquiry form, we may collect information such as your name, email address, selected service, company or business information, project requirements, and other information you choose to provide.

If you contact us through WhatsApp, Instagram, email, telephone, or another communication channel, we may receive the information you choose to provide through that service.

During a project, we may also receive information, files, account access, business data, website content, documents, or other materials necessary to provide the agreed services. We only request information reasonably necessary for the relevant project.

Why we process information

We may process information where reasonably necessary to:

  • respond to enquiries and requests;
  • understand project requirements;
  • prepare quotations, proposals, and project agreements;
  • communicate with prospective and existing clients;
  • deliver websites, automation, and related services;
  • provide customer support and maintenance;
  • process and confirm payments;
  • maintain appropriate business and accounting records;
  • prevent misuse, fraud, or unauthorized activity; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Form delivery

The website uses FormSubmit to deliver contact-form enquiries to GoOnline@g-7.shop. FormSubmit may process submitted information as necessary to provide its form-delivery service.

Please do not submit passwords, payment credentials, confidential security information, or other highly sensitive information through the enquiry form.

Payments

If you purchase services from G-7, payments may be processed through third-party payment providers such as Razorpay or other providers identified during the payment process.

We do not intentionally request or store full payment card details, UPI credentials, banking passwords, or similar payment credentials through this website. Payment information should be entered only through the payment provider's secure payment interface.

Payment providers may collect and process payment-related information according to their own privacy policies, terms, security practices, and applicable requirements.

Third-party services

G-7 may use third-party services to operate the website, communicate with clients, process enquiries, provide projects, or process payments. Depending on the service being provided, these may include:

  • FormSubmit — contact-form delivery
  • Google Apps Script and Google Sheets — portfolio or business-data processing
  • Razorpay or other payment providers — payment processing
  • WhatsApp — customer communication
  • Instagram — communication and social presence
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting
  • other hosting, API, software, messaging, or cloud services required for a project

Third-party services have their own terms and privacy policies. We are not responsible for the independent privacy practices of third-party providers.

International processing

Some third-party service providers used by G-7 may process information on systems or servers located outside India. Where this occurs, the processing may be subject to the provider's own terms, privacy practices, security measures, and applicable legal requirements.

Cookies and browser storage

This website does not currently use non-essential advertising or analytics cookies.

The website uses session-based browser storage to remember whether the G-7 welcome animation has already been shown during your current browser session.

If we introduce analytics, advertising, additional cookies, or similar technologies in the future, this policy may be updated accordingly.

Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, manage business communications, provide services, maintain appropriate business records, meet legal or accounting obligations, prevent misuse, and resolve disputes.

When information is no longer reasonably required and there is no legal or legitimate business reason to retain it, we may delete or securely dispose of it.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure. However, no internet transmission, website, third-party service, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Payment card details and other payment credentials should only be entered into the payment provider's secure payment interface.

Your choices and rights

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have submitted, subject to applicable law and legitimate business, legal, accounting, security, or dispute-resolution requirements.

We may need to verify the identity of a person making a request before providing access to or changing personal information.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for businesses, professionals, and other customers seeking digital services. It is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, third-party providers, or legal obligations change.
